3 Box the text and type what it says

Drag across a piece of text to box it, type what it says, and press Enter to finish it and draw the next. A box with no label yet is drawn dashed grey. Click a box to edit it, drag its middle to move it or a corner to resize it. Esc deselects, Delete removes the selected box, and Ctrl+Z takes back the last one drawn.
